## 6. Finding format (freeze-aware — there is NO audit-reconciliation ship)

For every finding:

- **Severity:** Critical / High / Medium / Low / Observation.
- **Ownership:** `fwd` (→ Reviewer binding-pass + operator) or `clif`
  (→ operator + the sibling clif agent; the Reviewer does not own or
  edit clif).
- **Disposition lane** (because the standing freeze permits no audit
  ship — a finding can only travel one of these): `verified-correctness`
  (may ride a permitted ship once code-verified) | `named-deferred-phase`
  (Core-#18 marker, Phase 9/10) | `doctrine-honesty` (a claim must be
  made honest or marked unverified — no code) | `out-of-scope`
  (consumer/operator decision) | `STOP-SHIP` (blocks v1.0.0 / freeze
  lift).
- Evidence: file:line or tx hash. No claim without it.

**Do not recommend an audit/reconciliation/scope-ceremony ship** — that
ship type does not exist under the standing rule; saying "ship a
reconciliation pass" is itself a finding *against* you. Recommendations
must fit the permitted lanes or be an explicit operator decision.

End with: the §2 verdicts A–E, the §0 ruling, and a one-paragraph
bottom line the operator can act on in one read.
