## What shipped

`POST /v1/sign-fsp-message`: EIP-191 personal-sign of a fwd-reconstructed FSP
messageHash for UPTIME and REWARD_DISTRIBUTION protocol messages, plus the
full authorization envelope that makes the endpoint safe (it could not ship
without it). No nonce, no broadcast, no receipt — RFC6979-deterministic,
idempotency unnecessary.

## Key design decisions

- **Dedicated `fsp_rate_buckets`** (not a `rate_buckets` `bucket_kind`
  column): SQLite cannot ALTER into a composite PK and env.py has no batch
  mode; the sibling table is the honest additive design.
- **Address-level key-domain segmentation** at two layers (comprehensive
  startup + cheap runtime belt-and-suspenders).
- **FSP-caller provisioning fix**: Check 1c would have boot-killed any
  fsp-only caller; now accepts `permissions` OR `fsp_permissions`.
