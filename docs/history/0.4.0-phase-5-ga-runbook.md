## What the runbook verifies — and what it doesn't

The runbook's 10-step procedure verifies **Scenario 1: 10-concurrent monotonic nonces on Coston2**. Pass criteria are explicit:

1. Receipt watcher started (log scan).
2. 10 distinct sequential nonces returned (no gaps, no duplicates).
3. All 10 transition to `status=mined` within 60 seconds.
4. First on-chain hash recovers to the wallet address.
5. `nonces.next_nonce` advanced by exactly 10; `last_confirmed` advanced by 9.
6. Coston2 explorer confirms 10 outgoing txs at the address.

**Scenario 2 — live low-gas replacement on Coston2** — is deferred. The watcher's replacement contract has unit coverage at v0.4.0a6 (`test_replace_tip_bump_math` proves the math; `test_process_tx_replaces_when_stuck_under_cap` proves the state-machine wiring; `test_watch_receipts_cancellation_propagates_cleanly` proves clean shutdown). Live replacement requires either making `_DEFAULT_TIP_WEI` env-configurable (a 5-line change that the runbook documents in the "Scenario 2 (deferred)" section) OR a fragile timing-based test that depends on chain conditions. The unit gate is sufficient for v0.4.0 GA; live replacement is a stretch goal that lands in a Phase 5 follow-up or Phase 7 ops runbook work.
